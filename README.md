# Tata Steel Ltd. – Financial Modeling & Valuation

## Project Overview
A comprehensive financial modeling and valuation project on **Tata Steel Ltd.** developed using Microsoft Excel.
The project covers historical financial statement analysis, ratio analysis, financial forecasting, cash flow analysis, WACC calculation, beta analysis, common-size financial statements, intrinsic growth analysis, DCF valuation, and relative pricing.

## Objectives
- Analyze Tata Steel's historical financial performance
- Build an integrated financial model
- Perform financial ratio analysis
- Forecast future financial performance
- Analyze operating, investing, and financing cash flows
- Calculate Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC)
- Analyze beta and market returns
- Perform common-size financial statement analysis
- Calculate Return on Invested Capital (ROIC) and intrinsic growth
- Estimate Free Cash Flow to Firm (FCFF)
- Perform Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) valuation
- Perform relative pricing / comparable company valuation

## Analysis Performed

Historical financial statements were analyzed to understand the company's financial performance and position.

- Income Statement
- Balance Sheet
- Cash Flow Statement
- Revenue and Revenue Growth
- COGS
- Gross Profit and Gross Margin
- EBITDA and EBITDA Margin
- EBIT
- Net Profit and Net Profit Margin
- EPS
- Dividend Per Share
- Retained Earnings

### 2. Ratio Analysis
Key financial ratios were calculated and analyzed to evaluate the company's financial health and operating performance.
- Sales Growth

- EBIT Growth

- Current Ratio

- Interest Coverage Ratio
- Gross Margin
- EBITDA Margin

- ROE
- ROA
- ROCE
- Asset Turnover
- Inventory Turnover
- Receivables Turnover

**Financing Activities**

### 5. WACC & Beta Analysis
The project includes a WACC calculation and market-based beta analysis.
The WACC analysis considers:
- Cost of Equity
- Cost of Debt
- Capital Structure
- Debt Weight
- Equity Weight
- Tax Rate
- Beta
- Risk-Free Rate
- Market Risk Premium

Beta analysis uses historical monthly market data to analyze the relationship between **Jindal Steel's returns and Nifty returns**.

### 6. Common Size Analysis
Common-size financial statements were prepared to analyze the composition of Tata Steel's:
- Income Statement
- Balance Sheet

### 7. Intrinsic Growth Analysis
Intrinsic growth analysis was performed using fundamental financial drivers including:

- NOPAT

- Intrinsic Growth

### 8. DCF Valuation
A Discounted Cash Flow valuation was developed to estimate the intrinsic value of Tata Steel Ltd.
The DCF model includes:
- EBIT
- Tax
- NOPAT
- Reinvestment
- FCFF
- Discount Rate
- Present Value of FCFF
- Terminal Value
- Enterprise Value
- Equity Value
- Intrinsic Value

### 9. Relative Pricing
A comparable company valuation was also performed using selected companies from the steel industry.
The analysis includes:
- Tata Steel
- JSW Steel
- Jindal Steel
- SAIL
- Jindal Stainless

Market and financial data were used to compare valuation multiples and enterprise values across comparable companies.
